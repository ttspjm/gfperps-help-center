How payouts work

When you can request

You can request your first payout from the day after you get funded. After that, with Daily payouts on, which is the default, you can request a payout every 24 hours. With Daily payouts off, you can request one every 3 days.

You can't request a payout on Saturday or Sunday. Submit your request on a weekday.

How much

Each payout can be up to 5% of your initial balance in profit. That's $250 on a $5K account and $5,000 on a $100K account. The minimum request is $100.

Profit above the 5% cap stays in your account, and you can take it in your next payout.

Example: on a $10K account the cap is $500. You have $800 in profit, so you request $500. The other $300 stays in the account for your next payout.

A 3% payout fee comes off the final amount you receive. It's applied last, after the profit split and any deductions.

Example: on a $100K Legacy with the 80% split, a $5,000 payout is $4,000 after the split. The 3% fee is $120, so you receive $3,880.

Airdrop accounts

Each payout from an airdrop account is capped at 4% of the starting balance or $1,000, whichever is lower. On a $10K account that's $400 per payout. From $25K up, it's $1,000.

Once your payouts add up to $1,000, the account is closed and you can't request any more payouts from it.

If your plan's normal payout cap is lower, the lower cap applies.

Payout methods

We pay out via Rise, crypto, Skrill and bank transfer. Bank transfer is only available in Kenya, South Africa, Egypt, Nigeria, Côte d'Ivoire, Tanzania, Cameroon and Ghana.

The name on your Goat Funded Trader account has to match the name on your payout account.

Processing time

Most payouts are paid the same day. We guarantee you receive yours within 2 business days of your request. Weekends and public holidays don't count toward the 2 days.

Example: request on Monday and you have it by Wednesday at the latest. Request on Friday and you have it by Tuesday.

If we don't pay within 2 business days, we add a $1,000 bonus to your payout. The bonus doesn't apply when the delay comes from pending or incomplete KYC, or from our team needing more information from you.

On your first payout, identity verification (KYC) comes first, and the 2 business days start once it's complete. See Identity verification (KYC).

If your payout is past 2 business days, the cause is almost always incomplete KYC or a pending review with our risk team. Either way we'll have contacted you, so check your email and dashboard first.

Biggest win vs biggest loss

To request a payout, your biggest losing trade can't be bigger than your biggest winning trade. If it is, you can't request yet. It's not a breach. Keep trading until your biggest win is at least as big as your biggest loss, then request again.

A tie passes. Each partial close counts as its own trade. After each payout the check starts again with new trades only.

Example: your biggest win is $300 and your biggest loss is $450, so you can't request a payout. You then close a trade at +$500. Your biggest win is now bigger than your biggest loss, and you can request.

Goat: your first payout locks your drawdown

On Goat, your first payout request locks your maximum drawdown floor at your initial balance. On Legacy and Blitz the floor never moves. See Maximum drawdown.

Identity verification (KYC)

When it happens

You verify your identity (KYC) and sign our Trader Agreement when you request your first payout. You can start trading your funded account straight away, and nothing is needed until then.

How it works

The provider depends on the payout method you choose:

  • Crypto, Skrill or bank transfer. You verify through Veriff. A Veriff link is sent to you when you request the payout. If Veriff can't verify you, we invite you to verify through Rise, and your payout still goes out by the method you chose.
  • Rise. You verify on the Rise platform. If Rise can't verify you, we offer Veriff instead. In that case your payout method changes to crypto or Skrill, because a Rise payout needs Rise verification.

If neither provider can verify you, we can't process the payout. Our support team will contact you with the next steps.

Tips for a quick check

  • Make sure the name on your Goat Funded Trader account matches your ID and your payout account.
  • Use a clear, valid, government-issued ID.
  • Complete it as soon as you're asked. Your payout's 2 business days start once verification is done.

Do I need to verify my identity (KYC) to buy or trade a challenge?

No. You don't need KYC to buy a challenge, trade your evaluation or trade your funded account.

You verify your identity and sign the Trader Agreement when you request your first payout. See Identity verification (KYC).
