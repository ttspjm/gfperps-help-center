Getting started

What Goat Funded Perps is, the three challenges, prices and the options you can set at checkout.

What is Goat Funded Perps?

Goat Funded Perps is Goat Funded Trader's program for perpetual contracts, or perps. You trade crypto, indices, commodities, forex and stocks on the Arca platform, with simulated capital, so your own money is never at risk on a position. Pass a 1-Step evaluation and you get a funded account that pays you a share of the profit you make.

How to get started

  1. Log in to your Goat Funded Trader dashboard. Goat Funded Perps uses the same login, so you don't need a new account.
  2. Switch to Perps using the product selector in your dashboard.
  3. Pick a challenge and an account size, set your options and buy it.

The path to a payout

  1. Reach the profit target in your 1-Step evaluation without breaking a drawdown limit or a trading rule. There's no time limit and no minimum number of trading days.
  2. Trade your funded account.
  3. From the day after you get funded, request a payout every 24 hours, or every 3 days if you turned Daily payouts off. Each payout can be up to 5% of your initial balance, and you keep 60% to 90% of it depending on your challenge and options. Your first payout includes a quick identity check (KYC).

Challenge options: profit split, Boosted leverage and Daily payouts

You choose these options when you buy a challenge. Your checkout shows the price for the combination you pick.

Boosted leverage

On by default. It raises leverage on stocks, indices, commodities, forex and seven crypto markets: BTC, ETH, SOL, ZEC, HYPE, NEAR and XRP. On every other crypto market leverage is 2x with or without it. Turn it off for standard leverage and a price about 5% lower. See Leverage and maximum position size for every market.

Daily payouts

On by default, so you can request a payout every 24 hours. Turn it off and you can request one every 3 days, at a price about 5% lower.

Either way, you can't request a payout on Saturday or Sunday. Submit your request on a weekday.

Profit split

Legacy and Blitz pay 80% of your profit. The 90% split adds 20% to the price.

Goat starts at 60%. You can raise it to 70%, 80% or 90%. Each step adds 10% to the price, on top of the step before.

Example: a $10K Goat costs $34 at 60%, $37.40 at 70%, $41.14 at 80% and $45.25 at 90%.

What payment options can I use to buy a challenge?

You can pay by card, digital wallet or crypto. Checkout has four options:

  • Credit / Debit card. Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay and Google Pay.
  • Crypto / Card / PayPal / Kora / AstroPay. Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT, USDC, PayPal, Kora, Skrill and AstroPay.
  • Credit / Debit card (alternative processor). Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Use this if your card was declined on the first option.
  • Crypto. Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT and USDC.

Every purchase is a one-time payment. Nothing renews or charges your card again.

My payment was declined

  • Try the alternative card processor.
  • Check that your bank hasn't blocked the payment. Many banks decline first payments to prop firms, and a quick call or in-app approval usually fixes it.
  • Check that the billing details match your card exactly, including the postcode.
  • Paying with American Express? Use the first card option. The alternative processor doesn't take Amex.
  • Paying in crypto? Send the exact amount on the correct network.

Can I get a refund or reset my account?

Goat Funded Perps challenges can't be refunded, and you can't reset an account.

If your account fails, it closes. To keep trading, buy a new challenge.

Every Goat comes in $5K, $10K, $25K, $50K, $100K. The maximum profit per payout is 5% of your initial balance, the news profit cap is 1% of your initial balance per news release, the minimum holding time is 30 seconds and the payout fee is 3%. There's no time limit, no minimum number of trading days and no consistency rule.

See Challenge options for how each upgrade changes the price.

How the profit target works

The target counts open positions. The evaluation passes automatically the moment your equity reaches your target, even with trades still open.

Example: a $10K Goat with the 12% target passes when your equity reaches $11,200.

How the trailing drawdown works

Your maximum drawdown floor starts 3% (or 5%) of your initial balance below it. It only moves at the end of the trading day, and only up. It stops for good once it reaches your initial balance.

Example: on a $10K Goat with the 3% drawdown, your floor starts at $9,700. You end day one at $10,150, so the floor moves up to $9,850. You end day two at $10,050, so it stays at $9,850.

Your first payout request also locks the floor at your initial balance. See Maximum drawdown.
