Daily drawdown

The daily drawdown is the most your account can lose in one trading day. Legacy and Blitz use 3%. Goat uses 2%, or 3% if you chose that upgrade.

How it's calculated

At each daily reset we take your balance and set your limit that percentage below it. Open positions don't count when the limit is set. The limit stays the same until the next reset, and your equity, open positions included, has to stay above it.

Example on a $10K Legacy: at the reset your balance is $10,000 and you have an open trade $200 in profit. The limit is based on the $10,000 balance only. 3% of $10,000 is $300, so your limit for the day is $9,700.

The same account on a Goat with the 2% daily drawdown: 2% of $10,000 is $200, so the limit is $9,800.

When it resets

The daily reset is at 5 PM EST, every day.

Daily and maximum drawdown are separate

Your account also has a maximum drawdown floor. You have to stay above both. See Maximum drawdown.

Maximum drawdown

The maximum drawdown is the lowest your equity can go before the account fails. The distance is a percentage of your initial balance.

ChallengeMaximum drawdownFloor on a $10K account
Legacy6% static$9,400
Blitz3% static$9,700
Goat3% end-of-day trailing, or 5% as an upgradeStarts at $9,700, or $9,500 with 5%

Static: Legacy and Blitz

The floor is set when the account starts and never moves. Profit and payouts don't change it.

End-of-day trailing: Goat

The floor starts 3% (or 5%) of your initial balance below it. At the end of each trading day (5 PM EST) we take the higher of your balance and your equity. If that's a new high, the floor moves up and stays the same distance below it. The floor never moves down, and highs reached during the day and then lost don't move it.

Example on a $10K Goat with the 3% drawdown: the floor starts at $9,700. On day one your equity reaches $10,350 during the day and you end at $10,150. The floor moves to $9,850, based on where you ended the day.

Goat: where the floor stops

The floor never goes above your initial balance. Once it gets there, it locks and stops trailing.

Example: the same account later ends a day at $10,420. 3% below that would be $10,120, but the floor stops at $10,000 and stays there.

Your first payout request also locks the floor at your initial balance, even if it hasn't got there yet. From then on, if your equity falls to your initial balance, the account fails.

Is there a consistency rule, minimum trading days or an equity-based daily drawdown?

No. Legacy, Blitz and Goat have none of these:

  • A consistency rule
  • A minimum number of trading days
  • A time limit
  • An equity-based daily drawdown

Your daily drawdown limit is set from your balance at the 5 PM EST daily reset, not your equity. Open positions don't count when the limit is set. See Daily drawdown.

To pass, reach your profit target without going below your daily or maximum drawdown limit, and follow the trading rules.

Minimum holding time

Every challenge has a minimum holding time of 30 seconds, in the evaluation and when funded.

If you close a trade less than 30 seconds after opening it, any profit from that trade is removed. If the trade loses money, the loss stays on your account.

Each partial close counts as its own trade. If you close part of a position less than 30 seconds after opening it, the profit on that part is removed.

Example: you open a trade and close it 12 seconds later with $80 profit. The $80 is removed. Another trade closed after 20 seconds loses $50, and that $50 loss stays.

Is news trading allowed?

Yes. You can trade during news on every challenge, in the evaluation and when funded.

The 1% news cap

From 24 November 2026, the cap applies to each news release, not to each trade. Every trade you open or close within 5 minutes before or after a high-impact news release counts toward that release, and together those trades can make at most 1% of your initial balance. High-impact means a red folder event on Forex Factory.

This includes a trade you opened earlier that closes in the window, a pending order that triggers in the window, and trades closed by a stop loss or take profit. A trade that opens during one release and closes during another counts toward both.

When the profit is counted

We wait until every trade linked to the release is closed, even if one stays open for hours or until the next day. Then we add up the profit from those trades and compare it with the cap.

What happens above the cap

Only the profit above 1% is removed, and you keep the rest. It's not a breach and the account carries on.

Example on a $100K account: the cap is $1,000. A trade you opened earlier closes with $700 profit during a red folder release. You open a second trade in the window and close it an hour later with $800 profit. That's $1,500 on one release, so you keep $1,000 and $500 is removed.

What changed on 24 November 2026

Before 24 November 2026, the cap applied to each trade on its own. The new method applies to every payout request still under review on 24 November 2026. Payout requests already decided stay as they are.

Prohibited trading practices

These rules apply to every challenge, in the evaluation and when funded.

  • Copying other traders. You can copy trades between your own accounts. Copying trades from another trader or from an external account isn't allowed, and neither is trading on ideas from signal services, trading groups, social media or research reports. See Copy trading, EAs and automation.
  • Account sharing. Nobody else can trade your account. Renting an account or using a rented one isn't allowed. Shared devices, or anything that suggests an account isn't operated independently, may lead to a review by our Risk Team. See VPNs, VPSs and IP address changes.
  • VPNs, VPSs and proxies. No hosting or IP-masking service on the trading platform or in the Trader Dashboard. See VPNs, VPSs and IP address changes.
  • Group trading. No coordinated positions across linked accounts to create fake volume, offset exposure or hedge against another trader.
  • Hedging. You can't hold opposite positions on the same asset or on correlated assets, whatever their size. This applies inside one account, across your own accounts (an evaluation and a funded account included) and between your account and another trader's.
  • Exploiting execution. No high-frequency trading, latency arbitrage, hedge or scalping arbitrage, quote stuffing, spoofing, layering or wash trading. No trading on stale prices, pricing or platform errors, broken feeds or system gaps.
  • Insider information and front-running. No trading on non-public or insider information. No placing trades ahead of trades placed elsewhere.
  • Arbitrage against other accounts. No arbitrage between your account and another account, whether it's another Goat Funded Trader account or one at another firm or exchange.
  • Bought passing strategies. No third-party or off-the-shelf strategies, EAs or services sold to pass evaluations.
  • Trading that harms our partners. No trading that puts our relationship with an exchange or market maker at risk, or that causes regulatory problems for us, an exchange or a market maker.
  • Switching strategy between stages. Trade your funded account the way you passed the evaluation. Passing with an EA and then trading by hand when funded isn't allowed, and neither is the reverse.
  • Gap trading. Don't hold positions just to profit from a weekend or overnight gap. Follow the trading hours and price rules of each contract.
  • Abusing the simulated environment. No non-stop large-volume trading without a clear strategy behind it.
  • Abusing repeat purchases. Using repeat purchases, other identities, payment methods or accounts to exploit discounts or system rules isn't allowed.

VPNs, VPSs and IP address changes

You can't use a VPN, a VPS, a proxy or any other hosting or IP-masking service with your Goat Funded Perps account. The rule covers the trading platform and the Trader Dashboard, on every challenge, in the evaluation and when funded.

What isn't allowed

  • VPNs
  • VPSs
  • Proxies
  • Remote or hosted environments
  • Any other hosting or routing service used to reach the trading platform or the Trader Dashboard

If we detect one of these services on the trading platform or in the Trader Dashboard, the account is subject to a straight breach. Any payout generated while using it isn't eligible for payment.

IP address changes

The region you buy your challenge from and the region you log in and trade from should stay reasonably consistent over time.

If your IP address changes, you need to be able to justify it. Our Risk Team reviews unexplained changes of region, repeated IP changes and access from several locations at the same time. They may ask you for proof, such as:

  • Travel tickets or booking confirmations
  • Passport stamps or other travel documents
  • Video verification or other reasonable proof of location

If you can't show that you alone operated the account, we may breach or restrict the account, reject a payout or suspend you from our services.

Travelling

If you plan to log in or trade from another country or region, tell our support team before you go. That avoids an unnecessary review of your account.

Copy trading, EAs and automation

Copy trading

You can copy trades between your own accounts, across all account types. You can't copy trades from another trader or from an account outside Goat Funded Trader.

Trading on ideas from signal services, trading groups, social media or research reports isn't allowed either.

EAs and bots

You can use an EA only if you own its source code and the platform allows it. Rented EAs and third-party EAs you don't own the code for aren't allowed.

Other ordinary automation may be allowed once it's approved. Contact support before you run it.

Keep the same approach when funded

If you pass the evaluation with an EA, keep using it on the funded account. Switching between automated and manual trading from one stage to the next isn't allowed.
