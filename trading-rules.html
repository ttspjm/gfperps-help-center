Daily drawdown

The daily drawdown is the most your account can lose in one trading day. Legacy and Blitz use 3%. Goat uses 2%, or 3% if you chose that upgrade.

How it's calculated

At each daily reset we take the higher of your balance and your equity, and set your limit that percentage below it. The limit stays the same until the next reset. Your equity, open positions included, has to stay above it.

Example on a $10K Legacy: at the reset your balance is $10,000 and your equity is $10,200. 3% of $10,200 is $306, so your limit for the day is $9,894.

The same account on a Goat with the 2% daily drawdown: 2% of $10,200 is $204, so the limit is $9,996.

Daily and maximum drawdown are separate

Your account also has a maximum drawdown floor. You have to stay above both. See Maximum drawdown.

Maximum drawdown

The maximum drawdown is the lowest your equity can go before the account fails. The distance is a percentage of your initial balance.

ChallengeMaximum drawdownFloor on a $10K account
Legacy6% static$9,400
Blitz3% static$9,700
Goat3% end-of-day trailing, or 5% as an upgradeStarts at $9,700, or $9,500 with 5%

Static: Legacy and Blitz

The floor is set when the account starts and never moves. Profit and payouts don't change it.

End-of-day trailing: Goat

The floor starts 3% (or 5%) of your initial balance below it. At the end of each trading day, if your account ends at a new high, the floor moves up and stays the same distance below that high. It never moves down. Highs reached during the day and then lost don't move it.

Example on a $10K Goat with the 3% drawdown: the floor starts at $9,700. On day one your equity reaches $10,350 during the day and you end at $10,150. The floor moves to $9,850, based on where you ended the day.

Goat: your first payout locks the floor

When you request your first payout, the floor locks at your initial balance and stops trailing. From then on, if your equity falls to your initial balance, the account fails.

Is news trading allowed?

Yes. You can trade during news on every challenge, in the evaluation and when funded.

The 1% news cap

If you open or close a trade within 5 minutes before or after a high-impact news release, the most that trade can make is 1% of your initial balance. High-impact means a red folder event on Forex Factory.

This includes trades closed by a stop loss, a take profit or a pending order.

What happens above the cap

The cap applies to each trade on its own. Only the profit above 1% is removed, and you keep the rest. It's not a breach and the account carries on.

Example on a $100K account: the cap is $1,000. You open a trade 2 minutes before a red folder release and close it with $1,800 profit. You keep $1,000 and $800 is removed.

Prohibited trading practices

These rules apply to every challenge, in the evaluation and when funded.

  • Copying other traders. You can copy trades between your own accounts. Copying trades from another trader or from an external account isn't allowed. See Copy trading, EAs and automation.
  • Account sharing. Nobody else can trade your account. Renting an account or using a rented one isn't allowed.
  • Group trading. No coordinated positions across linked accounts to create fake volume, offset exposure or hedge against another trader.
  • Hedging. You can't hold opposite positions on the same asset, whatever their size. This applies inside one account, across your own accounts and between your account and another trader's.
  • Exploiting execution. No high-frequency trading, latency arbitrage, hedge or scalping arbitrage, quote stuffing, spoofing, layering or wash trading. No trading on stale prices, broken feeds or system gaps.
  • Insider information and front-running. No trading on non-public or insider information. No placing trades ahead of trades placed elsewhere.
  • All-or-nothing trading. No strategy where a single trade can lose the account.
  • Martingale. No raising your size after a loss to win back earlier losses, and no grid-based recovery systems.
  • Switching strategy between stages. Trade your funded account the way you passed the evaluation. Passing with an EA and then trading by hand when funded isn't allowed, and neither is the reverse.
  • Gap trading. Don't hold positions just to profit from a weekend or overnight gap. Follow the trading hours and price rules of each contract.
  • Trade flooding. No floods of tiny or unrealistic trades to inflate your trade count or results.
  • Abusing the simulated environment. No non-stop large-volume trading without a clear strategy behind it.
  • Getting around the Blitz purchase cap. You can buy Blitz again up to its cap. Using other identities, payment methods or accounts to buy more, or using repeat purchases to exploit discounts or system rules, isn't allowed.

Copy trading, EAs and automation

Copy trading

You can copy trades between your own accounts, across all account types. You can't copy trades from another trader or from an account outside Goat Funded Trader.

EAs and bots

You can use an EA only if you own its source code and the platform allows it. Rented EAs and third-party EAs you don't own the code for aren't allowed.

Other ordinary automation may be allowed once it's approved. Contact support before you run it.

Keep the same approach when funded

If you pass the evaluation with an EA, keep using it on the funded account. Switching between automated and manual trading from one stage to the next isn't allowed.
