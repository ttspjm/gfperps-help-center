Legacy challenge rules

Updated 23 November 2026

Legacy has the widest drawdown of the three challenges. It's built to be the easiest to pass.

RuleLegacy
Type1-Step
Account sizes$5K, $10K, $25K, $50K, $100K
Profit target10%, counts open positions
Daily drawdown3%
Maximum drawdown6% static
Time limitNone
Minimum trading daysNone
Consistency ruleNone
Profit split80%, or 90% as an option
PayoutsEvery 24 hours, or every 3 days with Daily payouts off
Maximum profit per payout5% of your initial balance
News profit cap1% of your initial balance per news release
Minimum holding time30 seconds
Payout fee3%
LeverageBoosted by default, standard with Boosted leverage off

How the profit target works

Legacy's target counts open positions. The evaluation passes automatically the moment your equity reaches 10% above your initial balance, even with trades still open.

Example: a $10K Legacy passes when your equity reaches $11,000.

Your limits on a $10K account

Your maximum drawdown floor is $9,400 and it never moves. Your daily limit is set 3% below your balance at each daily reset. See Daily drawdown and Maximum drawdown.
