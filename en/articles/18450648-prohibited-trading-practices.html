Prohibited trading practices

Updated 15 November 2026

These rules apply to every challenge, in the evaluation and when funded.

  • Copying other traders. You can copy trades between your own accounts. Copying trades from another trader or from an external account isn't allowed. See Copy trading, EAs and automation.
  • Account sharing. Nobody else can trade your account. Renting an account or using a rented one isn't allowed.
  • Group trading. No coordinated positions across linked accounts to create fake volume, offset exposure or hedge against another trader.
  • Hedging. You can't hold opposite positions on the same asset, whatever their size. This applies inside one account, across your own accounts and between your account and another trader's.
  • Exploiting execution. No high-frequency trading, latency arbitrage, hedge or scalping arbitrage, quote stuffing, spoofing, layering or wash trading. No trading on stale prices, broken feeds or system gaps.
  • Insider information and front-running. No trading on non-public or insider information. No placing trades ahead of trades placed elsewhere.
  • All-or-nothing trading. No strategy where a single trade can lose the account.
  • Martingale. No raising your size after a loss to win back earlier losses, and no grid-based recovery systems.
  • Switching strategy between stages. Trade your funded account the way you passed the evaluation. Passing with an EA and then trading by hand when funded isn't allowed, and neither is the reverse.
  • Gap trading. Don't hold positions just to profit from a weekend or overnight gap. Follow the trading hours and price rules of each contract.
  • Trade flooding. No floods of tiny or unrealistic trades to inflate your trade count or results.
  • Abusing the simulated environment. No non-stop large-volume trading without a clear strategy behind it.
  • Getting around the Blitz purchase cap. You can buy Blitz again up to its cap. Using other identities, payment methods or accounts to buy more, or using repeat purchases to exploit discounts or system rules, isn't allowed.
