Prohibited trading practices

Updated 26 November 2026

These rules apply to every challenge, in the evaluation and when funded.

  • Copying other traders. You can copy trades between your own accounts. Copying trades from another trader or from an external account isn't allowed, and neither is trading on ideas from signal services, trading groups, social media or research reports. See Copy trading, EAs and automation.
  • Account sharing. Nobody else can trade your account. Renting an account or using a rented one isn't allowed. Shared devices, or anything that suggests an account isn't operated independently, may lead to a review by our Risk Team. See VPNs, VPSs and IP address changes.
  • VPNs, VPSs and proxies. No hosting or IP-masking service on the trading platform or in the Trader Dashboard. See VPNs, VPSs and IP address changes.
  • Group trading. No coordinated positions across linked accounts to create fake volume, offset exposure or hedge against another trader.
  • Hedging. You can't hold opposite positions on the same asset or on correlated assets, whatever their size. This applies inside one account, across your own accounts (an evaluation and a funded account included) and between your account and another trader's.
  • Exploiting execution. No high-frequency trading, latency arbitrage, hedge or scalping arbitrage, quote stuffing, spoofing, layering or wash trading. No trading on stale prices, pricing or platform errors, broken feeds or system gaps.
  • Insider information and front-running. No trading on non-public or insider information. No placing trades ahead of trades placed elsewhere.
  • Arbitrage against other accounts. No arbitrage between your account and another account, whether it's another Goat Funded Trader account or one at another firm or exchange.
  • Bought passing strategies. No third-party or off-the-shelf strategies, EAs or services sold to pass evaluations.
  • Trading that harms our partners. No trading that puts our relationship with an exchange or market maker at risk, or that causes regulatory problems for us, an exchange or a market maker.
  • Switching strategy between stages. Trade your funded account the way you passed the evaluation. Passing with an EA and then trading by hand when funded isn't allowed, and neither is the reverse.
  • Gap trading. Don't hold positions just to profit from a weekend or overnight gap. Follow the trading hours and price rules of each contract.
  • Trade flooding. No floods of tiny or unrealistic trades to inflate your trade count or results.
  • Abusing the simulated environment. No non-stop large-volume trading without a clear strategy behind it.
  • Abusing repeat purchases. Using repeat purchases, other identities, payment methods or accounts to exploit discounts or system rules isn't allowed.
