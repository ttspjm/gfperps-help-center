Is the daily drawdown based on my balance or my equity?

Updated 26 November 2026

Your balance. Your daily drawdown limit is set once a day from your balance at the 5 PM EST reset. Open trades don't count when the limit is set, so profit on them never raises it.

The limit is 3% on Legacy and Blitz. On Goat it's 2%, or 3% if you chose that upgrade. It stays where it is until the next reset.

Example

On a $10K Legacy, your balance at the reset is $10,000 and you have a trade open $400 in profit. Your limit for the day is 3% below the $10,000 balance, which is $9,700. Your equity is $10,400, so that trade can give back all of its profit and you still have $300 of room.

Your maximum drawdown is separate

You also have to stay above your maximum drawdown floor. See Does my maximum drawdown trail or lock at my initial balance after my first payout?
