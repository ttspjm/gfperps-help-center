Is there a consistency rule, minimum trading days or an equity-based daily drawdown?

Updated 17 November 2026

No. Legacy, Blitz and Goat have none of these:

  • A consistency rule
  • A minimum number of trading days
  • A time limit
  • An equity-based daily drawdown

Your daily drawdown limit is set from your balance at the 5 PM EST daily reset, not your equity. Open positions don't count when the limit is set. See Daily drawdown.

To pass, reach your profit target without going below your daily or maximum drawdown limit, and follow the trading rules.
