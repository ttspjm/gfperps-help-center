Account sizes and prices

Updated 15 November 2026

Every challenge comes in five sizes. The prices below include Boosted leverage and Daily payouts, which are both on by default. Your checkout shows the final price for the options you pick.

Account sizeLegacyBlitzGoat (starting price)
$5K$58$28$14
$10K$118$64$34
$25K$278$144$78
$50K$498$234$148
$100K$988$434$278

Turning options off lowers the price

Turning off Boosted leverage or Daily payouts takes about 5% off the price for each one you turn off.

Example: a $100K Legacy costs $988 with both on, $940.95 with one of them off and $898.18 with both off.

Options that raise the price

On Legacy and Blitz, the 90% profit split adds 20% to the price.

Goat's starting price is its cheapest setup: 12% profit target, 2% daily drawdown, 3% maximum drawdown and a 60% profit split. Each upgrade adds to that price. See Challenge options for the full list.
