Can I get a refund or reset my account?

Updated 25 November 2026

Goat Funded Perps challenges can't be refunded. All purchases are final.

That includes a challenge you haven't traded yet, one that fails, and an account closed for prohibited trading practices.

The fee isn't refunded with a payout either, including your fourth payout.

Resets

You can reset an evaluation account once, whether it's still active or has failed. A reset costs 2% less than you paid for the original challenge, after any coupon.

If your account has failed, you have 14 calendar days from the day it failed to buy the reset. After that the reset option is gone, and you'll need to buy a new challenge.

Funded accounts can't be reset.

If your evaluation fails again after you've used your reset, the account closes. To keep trading, buy a new challenge.
