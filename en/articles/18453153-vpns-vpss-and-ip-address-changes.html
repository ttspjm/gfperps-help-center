VPNs, VPSs and IP address changes

Updated 16 November 2026

You can't use a VPN, a VPS, a proxy or any other hosting or IP-masking service with your Goat Funded Perps account. The rule covers the trading platform and the Trader Dashboard, on every challenge, in the evaluation and when funded.

What isn't allowed

  • VPNs
  • VPSs
  • Proxies
  • Remote or hosted environments
  • Any other hosting or routing service used to reach the trading platform or the Trader Dashboard

If we detect one of these services on the trading platform or in the Trader Dashboard, the account is subject to a straight breach. Any payout generated while using it isn't eligible for payment.

IP address changes

The region you buy your challenge from and the region you log in and trade from should stay reasonably consistent over time.

If your IP address changes, you need to be able to justify it. Our Risk Team reviews unexplained changes of region, repeated IP changes and access from several locations at the same time. They may ask you for proof, such as:

  • Travel tickets or booking confirmations
  • Passport stamps or other travel documents
  • Video verification or other reasonable proof of location

If you can't show that you alone operated the account, we may breach or restrict the account, reject a payout or suspend you from our services.

Travelling

If you plan to log in or trade from another country or region, tell our support team before you go. That avoids an unnecessary review of your account.
