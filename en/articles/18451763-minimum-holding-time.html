Minimum holding time

Updated 27 November 2026

The minimum holding time of 30 seconds applies to funded accounts only. In the evaluation, every trade counts in full, however long you hold it.

If you close a trade less than 30 seconds after opening it, you keep up to 1% of your initial balance in profit from that trade. We remove any profit above 1%. A loss always stays on your account.

The limit is per trade. Each partial close counts as its own trade.

Example: on a $10K funded account, 1% is $100. You close a trade 12 seconds after opening it with $150 profit. You keep $100 and we remove $50. Another trade closed after 20 seconds with $60 profit keeps all $60. A trade closed after 15 seconds with a $50 loss keeps the loss.
