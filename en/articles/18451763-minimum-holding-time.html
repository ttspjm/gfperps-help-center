Minimum holding time

Updated 15 November 2026

Every challenge has a minimum holding time of 30 seconds.

If you close a trade less than 30 seconds after opening it, any profit from that trade is removed. If the trade loses money, the loss stays on your account.

Example: you open a trade and close it 12 seconds later with $80 profit. The $80 is removed. Another trade closed after 20 seconds loses $50, and that $50 loss stays.
