Maximum allocation

Updated 17 November 2026

Your allocation is the combined size of your Goat Funded Perps accounts. These are the most you can hold:

StageMaximum allocation
Evaluation$500K
Funded$200K

Countries with a $100K cap

Traders in Malaysia, India, Pakistan, Indonesia and Thailand can hold up to $100K in funded accounts. The evaluation limit is the same as everyone else's, $500K.

Example: two $50K funded accounts reach the $100K limit. You can still hold up to $500K in evaluations.
