Trading and maintenance fees

Updated 15 November 2026

All three challenges have the same fees.

FeeRateWhen it's charged
Trading fee0.045% per sideWhen you open a position, and again when you close it
Maintenance fee0.0015% per hour (0.036% per day)Every hour a position stays open

Example: a $10,000 position held for one day costs $4.50 to open, $4.50 to close and $3.60 in maintenance fees, $12.60 in total.
