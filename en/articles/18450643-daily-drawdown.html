Daily drawdown

Updated 15 November 2026

The daily drawdown is the most your account can lose in one trading day. Legacy and Blitz use 3%. Goat uses 2%, or 3% if you chose that upgrade.

How it's calculated

At each daily reset we take your balance and set your limit that percentage below it. Open positions don't count when the limit is set. The limit stays the same until the next reset, and your equity, open positions included, has to stay above it.

Example on a $10K Legacy: at the reset your balance is $10,000 and you have an open trade $200 in profit. The limit is based on the $10,000 balance only. 3% of $10,000 is $300, so your limit for the day is $9,700.

The same account on a Goat with the 2% daily drawdown: 2% of $10,000 is $200, so the limit is $9,800.

When it resets

The daily reset is at 5 PM EST, every day.

Daily and maximum drawdown are separate

Your account also has a maximum drawdown floor. You have to stay above both. See Maximum drawdown.
