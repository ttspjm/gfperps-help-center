Blitz challenge rules

Updated 23 November 2026

Blitz is the cheapest of the fixed challenges and has the tightest drawdown.

RuleBlitz
Type1-Step
Account sizes$5K, $10K, $25K, $50K, $100K
Profit target9%, counts open positions
Daily drawdown3%
Maximum drawdown3% static
Time limitNone
Minimum trading daysNone
Consistency ruleNone
Profit split80%, or 90% as an option
PayoutsEvery 24 hours, or every 3 days with Daily payouts off
Maximum profit per payout5% of your initial balance
News profit cap1% of your initial balance per news release
Minimum holding time30 seconds
Payout fee3%
LeverageBoosted by default, standard with Boosted leverage off

How the profit target works

The target counts open positions. The evaluation passes automatically the moment your equity reaches 9% above your initial balance, even with trades still open.

Example: a $10K Blitz passes when your equity reaches $10,900.

Your limits on a $10K account

Your maximum drawdown floor is $9,700 and it never moves. On a new account the daily limit sits at the same level, so on day one the two limits are the same line. Once you're in profit, the daily limit moves up with you and becomes the one to watch.
