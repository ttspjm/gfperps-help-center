Do I need to verify my identity (KYC) to buy or trade a challenge?

Updated 17 November 2026

No. You don't need KYC to buy a challenge, trade your evaluation or trade your funded account.

You verify your identity and sign the Trader Agreement when you request your first payout. See Identity verification (KYC).
