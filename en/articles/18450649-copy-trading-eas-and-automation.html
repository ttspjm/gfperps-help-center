Copy trading, EAs and automation

Updated 27 November 2026

Copy trading

You can copy trades between your own accounts, across all account types. You can't copy trades from another trader or from an account outside Goat Funded Trader.

Trading on ideas from signal services, trading groups, social media or research reports isn't allowed either.

EAs and bots

You can use an EA only if you own its source code and the platform allows it. Rented EAs and third-party EAs you don't own the code for aren't allowed.

Other ordinary automation may be allowed once it's approved. Contact support before you run it.
