Identity verification (KYC)

Updated 17 November 2026

When it happens

You verify your identity (KYC) and sign our Trader Agreement when you request your first payout. You can start trading your funded account straight away, and nothing is needed until then.

How it works

The provider depends on the payout method you choose:

  • Crypto, Skrill or bank transfer. You verify through Veriff. A Veriff link is sent to you when you request the payout. If Veriff can't verify you, we invite you to verify through Rise, and your payout still goes out by the method you chose.
  • Rise. You verify on the Rise platform. If Rise can't verify you, we offer Veriff instead. In that case your payout method changes to crypto or Skrill, because a Rise payout needs Rise verification.

If neither provider can verify you, we can't process the payout. Our support team will contact you with the next steps.

Tips for a quick check

  • Make sure the name on your Goat Funded Trader account matches your ID and your payout account.
  • Use a clear, valid, government-issued ID.
  • Complete it as soon as you're asked. Your payout's 24 hours start once verification is done.
