Leverage and maximum position size

Updated 16 November 2026

Every challenge has Boosted leverage on by default. If you turn it off at checkout, you get standard leverage and the price drops by about 5%.

It raises leverage on stocks, indices, commodities, forex and seven crypto markets: BTC, ETH, SOL, ZEC, HYPE, NEAR and XRP. On every other crypto market leverage is 2x with or without it.
