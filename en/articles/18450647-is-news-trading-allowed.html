Is news trading allowed?

Updated 23 November 2026

Yes. You can trade during news on every challenge, in the evaluation and when funded.

The 1% news cap

From 24 November 2026, the cap applies to each news release, not to each trade. Every trade you open or close within 5 minutes before or after a high-impact news release counts toward that release, and together those trades can make at most 1% of your initial balance. High-impact means a red folder event on Forex Factory.

This includes a trade you opened earlier that closes in the window, a pending order that triggers in the window, and trades closed by a stop loss or take profit. A trade that opens during one release and closes during another counts toward both.

When the profit is counted

We wait until every trade linked to the release is closed, even if one stays open for hours or until the next day. Then we add up the profit from those trades and compare it with the cap.

What happens above the cap

Only the profit above 1% is removed, and you keep the rest. It's not a breach and the account carries on.

Example on a $100K account: the cap is $1,000. A trade you opened earlier closes with $700 profit during a red folder release. You open a second trade in the window and close it an hour later with $800 profit. That's $1,500 on one release, so you keep $1,000 and $500 is removed.

What changed on 24 November 2026

Before 24 November 2026, the cap applied to each trade on its own. The new method applies to every payout request still under review on 24 November 2026. Payout requests already decided stay as they are.
