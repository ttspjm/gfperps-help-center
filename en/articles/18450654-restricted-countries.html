Restricted countries

Updated 15 November 2026

You can't buy or trade a Goat Funded Perps account if you're in:

  • Vietnam
  • Cambodia
  • The United Arab Emirates
  • Taiwan
  • Any country on the US sanctions list

Malaysia, India, Pakistan, Indonesia and Thailand are allowed with a $100K maximum allocation. Every other country is allowed.
