Does my maximum drawdown trail or lock at my initial balance after my first payout?

Updated 26 November 2026

It depends on your challenge.

ChallengeDoes it trail?Locks at initial balance after first payout?Floor on a $10K account
LegacyNo, staticNo$9,400, always
BlitzNo, staticNo$9,700, always
GoatYes, end-of-day trailingYesStarts at $9,700, or $9,500 with the 5% upgrade. Stops at $10,000

Legacy and Blitz: your floor never moves

Your floor is set the day the account starts and stays there. It doesn't follow your profit, and a payout doesn't lock it at your initial balance.

Example: on a $10K Legacy your floor is $9,400. Grow the account to $11,000 and take payouts, and it's still $9,400.

Goat: your floor trails, then locks

Goat's floor starts 3% below your initial balance, or 5% if you chose that upgrade. At the end of each trading day, 5 PM EST, it moves up with any new end-of-day high and stays the same distance below it. It never moves down.

It stops trailing and locks at your initial balance as soon as one of these happens:

  • It reaches your initial balance
  • You request your first payout

Example on a $10K Goat with the 3% drawdown: the floor starts at $9,700. You end a day at $10,150, so it moves to $9,850. When you request your first payout, it locks at $10,000. From then on, if your equity falls to $10,000, the account fails.
