Maximum drawdown

Updated 15 November 2026

The maximum drawdown is the lowest your equity can go before the account fails. The distance is a percentage of your initial balance.

ChallengeMaximum drawdownFloor on a $10K account
Legacy6% static$9,400
Blitz3% static$9,700
Goat3% end-of-day trailing, or 5% as an upgradeStarts at $9,700, or $9,500 with 5%

Static: Legacy and Blitz

The floor is set when the account starts and never moves. Profit and payouts don't change it.

End-of-day trailing: Goat

The floor starts 3% (or 5%) of your initial balance below it. At the end of each trading day, if your account ends at a new high, the floor moves up and stays the same distance below that high. It never moves down. Highs reached during the day and then lost don't move it.

Example on a $10K Goat with the 3% drawdown: the floor starts at $9,700. On day one your equity reaches $10,350 during the day and you end at $10,150. The floor moves to $9,850, based on where you ended the day.

Goat: your first payout locks the floor

When you request your first payout, the floor locks at your initial balance and stops trailing. From then on, if your equity falls to your initial balance, the account fails.
