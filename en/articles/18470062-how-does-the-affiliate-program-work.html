How does the affiliate program work?

Updated 23 November 2026

You earn commission on every sale made through your affiliate link. You also earn 5% of the sales made by affiliates you invite, called sub-affiliates.

How to join

You apply from your Goat Funded Trader dashboard. Goat Funded Perps uses the same dashboard and login.

  1. Log in to your Goat Funded Trader dashboard. If you don't have an account yet, create one first.
  2. Open the Affiliate section.
  3. Fill in the application form with all the required details.

Our team reviews every application. Once you're approved, you'll find your affiliate link and your invite link in the Affiliate section.

If another affiliate invited you, apply through their invite link. That makes you their sub-affiliate, and it doesn't reduce your own commission.

Your commission

  • 15% commission on every new customer you refer.
  • 10% commission on any later purchases those customers make.

Example: you refer Anna. She buys a $100 challenge, then another $100 challenge. You earn $15 on her first purchase and $10 on her second.

Challenge purchases and the options you add to them earn commission. Resets don't. Commission is calculated on the price the customer paid, after any discount. Your own purchases don't earn commission.

Three ways to earn commission

  • Your referral link. A purchase counts if it's made within 30 days of clicking your link.
  • Your coupon code. Every purchase made with your coupon code counts, with no time limit, even if the customer never clicked your link.
  • Your giveaways. Everyone who joins one of your giveaways is linked to you for 14 days. Their purchases in those 14 days count, even if they create an account first.

Your coupon code

Your coupon code stays the same, but its discount follows our current offer, so the % off can change. Every Monday, check the discount in the Coupons tab of your affiliate dashboard and make sure your content shows the right one.

Giveaways

You can run giveaways from the Giveaways tab in the Affiliate section of your Goat Funded Trader dashboard. Every sale from someone who joined your giveaway earns commission, the same as a sale through your link.

Sub-affiliate commission

Invite other creators, influencers or traders to apply to the affiliate program with your invite link. Everyone who is approved after applying through it becomes your sub-affiliate, and you earn 5% of the full amount of every sale they make.

  • Your sub-affiliates still earn their own full 15% or 10% on those sales. Your 5% doesn't come out of their commission.
  • You earn the 5% for as long as your sub-affiliate's account stays active. If their affiliate account is disabled for any reason, including fraud, the 5% stops.
  • Someone only becomes your sub-affiliate by applying through your invite link. An affiliate who already joined without it can't be linked to you unless they apply again through your link.
  • There is no limit on how many sub-affiliates you can invite.
  • The 5% covers one level only, so you don't earn on affiliates that your sub-affiliates invite.

Say you invite 10 creators and each of them makes $2,000 in sales this month. That's $20,000 in total, and 5% of it is $1,000 for you, without selling anything extra yourself.

When you can request your commission

You can request a commission 30 days after it's credited to your account. Each commission has its own 30 days. For example, commission credited on 13 November can be requested from 13 December.

  • The minimum payout request is $100. Only commission that has passed its 30 days counts toward it.
  • You need to complete identity verification (KYC) before your first payout.
  • Request your payout from the Affiliate section of your dashboard. Payouts are sent by crypto or Skrill and usually take 2 days to process.

Cookie tracking

We use a 30-day cookie. If someone clicks your link and buys within 30 days, the sale counts toward your commission. Purchases after those 30 days don't count, unless the customer uses your coupon code. For example, if someone clicks your link and buys on day 29 and again on day 31, only the first purchase earns commission.

Refunds and chargebacks

If a sale you referred is refunded or charged back, the commission on it is removed, even if its 30 days have already passed. If it was already paid out, it's deducted from your balance. We may disable the affiliate account of affiliates with a high number of refunds or chargebacks.

Paid ads

Running paid ads to earn commission is not allowed.
