Payouts

How often you can request a payout, how much you can take out and what can block a request.

  • How payouts work

    Request a payout every 24 hours, or every 3 days with Daily payouts off. Up to 5% of your initial balance, $100 minimum. Paid within 2 business days, most the same day. No requests on weekends.

  • Identity verification (KYC)

    You verify your identity and sign the Trader Agreement when you request your first payout, not when you get funded.
