Getting started

What Goat Funded Perps is, the three challenges, prices and the options you can set at checkout.

  • What is Goat Funded Perps?

    Trade perpetual contracts with simulated capital. Pick Legacy, Blitz or Goat, pass one evaluation step, then request payouts as often as every 24 hours.

  • Account sizes and prices

    All three challenges come in $5K, $10K, $25K, $50K and $100K. A $5K Goat starts at $12.

  • Challenge options: profit split, Boosted leverage and Daily payouts

    You set your options at checkout. Boosted leverage and Daily payouts are on by default, and turning them off lowers the price.

  • Legacy challenge rules

    1-Step with a 10% profit target, 3% daily drawdown and a 6% static maximum drawdown. Profit split 80%, or 90% as an option.

  • Blitz challenge rules

    1-Step with a 9% profit target, 3% daily drawdown and a 3% static maximum drawdown. The cheapest fixed challenge.

  • Goat challenge rules

    A 1-Step you set up yourself. It starts at a 12% target, 2% daily drawdown, 3% trailing drawdown and 60% split, and you can upgrade each one.
