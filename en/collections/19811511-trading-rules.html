Trading rules

Drawdown limits, leverage, fees, news trading and the practices that aren't allowed.

  • Daily drawdown

    Your daily limit is set once a day, below your balance at the reset. 3% on Legacy and Blitz, 2% or 3% on Goat.

  • Maximum drawdown

    Legacy and Blitz have a static floor that never moves. Goat's floor trails your end-of-day high and stops at your initial balance.

  • Leverage and maximum position size

    Leverage and maximum position size for all 100 markets. Boosted leverage is on by default, up to 25x on forex.

  • Trading and maintenance fees

    0.045% of the position value when you open and again when you close, plus 0.0015% per hour while a position is open.

  • Is news trading allowed?

    Yes. On a trade near high-impact news, profit above 1% of your initial balance is removed. It's not a breach.

  • Prohibited trading practices

    Hedging, martingale, account sharing and the other practices that aren't allowed on any account.

  • Copy trading, EAs and automation

    You can copy trades between your own accounts. Copying other traders and using EAs you don't own isn't allowed.

  • Maximum allocation

    Up to $500K in evaluations and $200K in funded accounts. Traders in five countries are capped at $100K in total.

  • Restricted countries

    Goat Funded Perps isn't available in Vietnam, Cambodia, the UAE, Taiwan or countries under US sanctions.

  • Minimum holding time

    Trades need to stay open for at least 30 seconds. Profit from a shorter trade is removed, but a loss stays.
